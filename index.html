SpaceStallionX Comment Responder

An automated workflow, built with n8n and AI, that replies to viewer comments on the SpaceStallionX YouTube channel and Facebook page.

  1. New comment arrives
  2. n8n filters spam and repeats
  3. AI writes a short reply
  4. Reply is posted and logged

1,100+ replies posted automatically on YouTube, with a log that prevents duplicate replies.
